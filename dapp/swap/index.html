One pool, two doors

The Tacit pool keeps one set of reserves for each pair. A wallet swaps with it in the open, like with any AMM. A private balance swaps with the same reserves through a proof, so both doors get the same price, and every trade through either one deepens the same pool.

The public door

An ordinary AMM contract: send ETH or a token, get the other side, in one transaction from your wallet. Because it is ordinary, routers reach it: zRouter sends ETH into it, and a large order is split between it and the Precision pool when that pays more.

The private door

Your balance is a set of notes only your key can open. A swap spends notes and makes new ones, proved on your device; the relay submits it and takes a small fee from the swap. The pool's reserves move by the trade's size, as they must, but nothing says whose swap it is or what else you hold.

From any token

zQuoter reads Uniswap, Sushi, Curve and zAMM for the best pool between your token and WETH. That leg buys exactly the WETH needed and hands it to PrecisionRoute, which unwraps it and buys the TAC: one transaction, never below the floor you saw. The part of your token not needed stays in your wallet.

Sign, don't approve

TAC, cUSD, cBTC, USDC, DAI and wstETH are spent with a permit: a free signature in your wallet, then one transaction. A token that already allows Permit2 signs a Permit2 transfer instead. Any other takes one approval, for the exact amount.
